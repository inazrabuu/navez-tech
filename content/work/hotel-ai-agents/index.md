challenge: |
  The hotel had good reviews and a loyal core of guests, but most bookings came through online travel agencies, with commission on every stay. Once guests checked out, the relationship ended: feedback forms were rarely returned, complaints surfaced first as public reviews, and there was no follow-up to bring guests back.

  Extra revenue depended on the front desk remembering to offer upgrades, transfers, spa treatments or dining, so most guests were never asked. Room rates were set from manual checks of competitors' prices a few times a week, which meant the hotel often reacted to market changes days late.
solution: |
  We designed and deployed three AI agents, each with a narrow job, clear limits and a person in charge of the decisions that matter.

  **1. Post-stay feedback and loyalty agent.** After checkout, the agent sends each guest a short, personal message by WhatsApp or email and collects their feedback in a conversation instead of a form.

  - Complaints go straight to the duty manager with the guest's stay details, so the team can recover the relationship before it becomes a public review.
  - Satisfied guests are invited to share a review and join the loyalty programme.
  - Returning guests get relevant offers at the right moment, such as their next business trip, a family holiday or an anniversary.

  **2. Upsell and cross-sell orchestration agent.** Before arrival and during the stay, the agent offers each guest the extras that fit their booking and history: room upgrades, early check-in, airport transfers, spa, dining and meeting rooms.

  - It coordinates timing across channels, so guests get a few well-placed offers instead of a stream of messages.
  - Accepted offers are confirmed back to the guest and recorded against the reservation for the team to fulfil.

  **3. Competitive monitoring agent.** Every day, the agent tracks competing hotels' public rates, availability, promotions and reviews.

  - It alerts the revenue manager when the market moves and suggests rate and promotion changes with the reasoning behind them.
  - The revenue manager decides; the agent never changes prices on its own.

  **Built-in guardrails.** Guests are contacted only with consent and can opt out at any time, in line with Indonesia's Personal Data Protection Law (UU PDP). Every message and recommendation is logged, and the hotel team can review and adjust each agent's rules.
