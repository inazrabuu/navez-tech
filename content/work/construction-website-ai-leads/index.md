challenge: |
  The company had built a solid reputation for commercial fit-outs, renovations and building maintenance, but almost all of its work came from referrals. Its website was a single page with a phone number, so prospects who searched online found competitors first, and there was no way to request a site survey or book a maintenance visit without calling during office hours.

  Enquiries that did come in arrived by phone, WhatsApp and email, and were tracked in the sales team's own notebooks and chats. Follow-ups depended on who remembered. Quotes went out days late, some leads were never contacted again, and invoices were typed up manually after each job was signed off, often a week or more later.
solution: |
  **1. A website built around the customer's questions.** We rebuilt the site around what facility managers, building owners and developers need to decide: services and project types, a portfolio of completed work, the process from survey to handover, and clear answers on timelines and pricing. Every page ends with a clear next step.

  **2. Online booking and enquiries.** Visitors can book a site survey, schedule a maintenance visit or request a quote online at any time. Each booking lands in the CRM with the project type, location and preferred dates, and the customer gets an instant confirmation.

  **3. Digital marketing that brings the right visitors.** We set up search-focused content and local listings for the services and areas the company actually serves, plus targeted search and social campaigns with tracking from first click to booked survey.

  **4. An AI agent that follows up and invoices.** We connected an AI agent to the CRM and the accounting system:

  - Every new enquiry is logged, qualified against the company's criteria and assigned to the right salesperson within minutes.
  - The agent sends confirmations and follow-ups by email and WhatsApp, reminds the team about open quotes, and flags leads that have gone quiet.
  - When a job or milestone is signed off, it prepares the invoice from the agreed quote, and a finance staff member approves it before it is sent.
  - Every action is logged in the CRM, so the team can see the full history of each customer.
