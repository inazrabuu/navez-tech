---
title: Stop re-typing supplier invoices and purchase documents
description: An AI agent that reads supplier invoices and delivery documents, matches them to purchase orders, and prepares entries for approval.
service: agentic-ai
industry: manufacturing
weight: 7
featured: false
problem: |
  The finance team receives supplier invoices as PDFs, scans and photos. Each one is typed into the accounting system and matched to a purchase order and goods receipt by hand. It's slow, error-prone and piles up at month-end.
approach: |
  - Build an agent that extracts key fields from incoming documents and checks them against the purchase order and goods receipt in the ERP.
  - Flag mismatches (quantity, price, tax details) for a person to review.
  - Prepare draft entries that a staff member approves before anything is posted.
outcome: |
  Less manual data entry, mismatches caught before payment, and finance staff reviewing exceptions instead of typing every document.
---
