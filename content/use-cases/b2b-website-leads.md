---
title: Turn a brochure website into a source of qualified leads
description: How a B2B company can turn a static brochure site into pages that answer buyer questions and route every enquiry to the right salesperson.
service: digital-agency
industry: manufacturing
weight: 2
featured: true
problem: |
  The company website was built years ago as an online brochure. It lists products but doesn't explain who they're for, and the only call to action is a generic contact form. Sales says "nobody finds us online", and enquiries that do arrive sit unanswered in a shared inbox.
approach: |
  - Interview sales and two or three customers to learn the questions buyers ask before they call.
  - Rebuild key pages around those questions: one page per product line or application, with specs, use cases and proof.
  - Add clear actions (book a call, chat on WhatsApp, request a quote) and connect each one to the CRM with an owner and a source.
  - Set up consent-based analytics to see which pages lead to enquiries.
outcome: |
  A site the sales team is happy to share, a measurable flow of enquiries with a named owner for each, and data showing which pages and channels bring in the right buyers.
faq:
  - q: Do we need to rebuild the whole site?
    a: Not always. We often start with the five to ten pages that matter most to buyers and improve the rest over time.
