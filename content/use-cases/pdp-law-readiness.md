---
title: Get ready for Indonesia's Personal Data Protection Law
description: A practical PDP Law readiness programme (data mapping, security controls, consent and breach response) run alongside your legal adviser.
service: cybersecurity
industry: cross-industry
weight: 8
featured: false
problem: |
  The company collects customer and employee data across its website, CRM, ERP and messaging tools. Management knows the PDP Law (UU No. 27/2022) now applies, but nobody has a clear list of what personal data is held, where, who can access it, or what to do if it leaks.
approach: |
  - Map personal data across systems: what is collected, why, where it is stored and who can see it.
  - Review security controls (access, encryption, backups, logging) against the risks found.
  - Update privacy notices and consent flows, starting with the website.
  - Write and rehearse a data breach response procedure.
  - Work alongside your legal adviser, who confirms legal interpretations.
outcome: |
  A documented picture of your personal data, practical controls in place, and a tested plan for responding to incidents, giving you evidence you can show customers, partners and regulators.
