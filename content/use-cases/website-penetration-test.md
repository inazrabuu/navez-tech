---
title: Pass a client's security review before signing a contract
description: A scoped penetration test and remediation of your website, portal or API, so you can answer an enterprise client's security questionnaire with evidence.
service: cybersecurity
industry: financial-services
weight: 9
featured: true
problem: |
  A large prospective client (a bank or multinational) sends a security questionnaire and asks for a recent penetration test report on the customer portal. The company has never had one, and the deal deadline is close.
approach: |
  - Agree scope, timing and rules of engagement in writing.
  - Test the portal, its APIs and login flows for common and business-logic vulnerabilities.
  - Report findings by severity with clear fixes, then help the team remediate.
  - Retest fixed issues and provide a summary letter suitable for sharing with the client.
outcome: |
  Real vulnerabilities fixed, a report and retest summary to share with the client, and confident answers to the security questionnaire.
---
