The law was enacted in October 2022 with a two-year transition period that ended in October 2024. It applies to organisations that process personal data of people in Indonesia, including many companies based outside the country.

## What does UU PDP cover?

- **Lawful basis** for processing personal data, including consent
- **Rights of data subjects**, such as access, correction and deletion
- **Obligations of controllers and processors**, including security measures and records of processing
- **Data breach notification** to affected people and the authorities within set time limits
- **Cross-border transfers** of personal data
- **Sanctions**, including administrative fines and criminal penalties for certain violations

## What does it mean in practice?

Companies need to know what personal data they hold and why, protect it with appropriate security controls, give people clear privacy information, and be ready to respond to incidents.
