Unlike an automated scan, a penetration test ("pen test") involves people who think like attackers. They combine tools with manual testing to find weaknesses such as broken access control, injection flaws, misconfigured cloud services or weak authentication, and show how they could be chained together.

## What are the common types?

- **Web application and API testing:** portals, websites and the APIs behind mobile apps
- **External network testing:** what is exposed to the internet
- **Internal network testing:** what an attacker could reach after getting inside
- **Cloud configuration review:** accounts, storage and identity settings

## What should a good pen test include?

1. Written **scope and rules of engagement**, agreed before any testing starts
2. Testing planned to avoid disrupting operations
3. A **report** with findings ranked by severity, evidence and clear fixes
4. A **retest** to confirm fixes work
