This policy explains how **PT. Navez Karsa Labs** ("navez.tech", "we") processes personal data when you visit navez.tech or contact us. We process personal data in line with Indonesia's Personal Data Protection Law (Undang-Undang No. 27 Tahun 2022 tentang Pelindungan Data Pribadi, "UU PDP").

## What we collect

- **Enquiry details** you give us through forms, booked calls, WhatsApp or email: name, work email, company, company size, the service you're interested in, budget range, timeline and your message.
- **Technical data** needed to run the site securely: IP address, browser type and pages requested, kept in short-lived server logs by our hosting provider.
- **Analytics data**, only if you consent: how you use the site, collected by HubSpot cookies.

## Why we process it (legal basis)

| Purpose | Data | Legal basis under UU PDP |
|---|---|---|
| Reply to your enquiry and arrange calls | Enquiry details | Your request before entering a contract; legitimate interest |
| Run and secure the website | Technical data | Legitimate interest |
| Understand site usage | Analytics data | Your explicit consent |

## Cookies and consent

Essential cookies are always on; they remember your consent choice. Analytics cookies (HubSpot) load **only after** you accept them in the cookie banner. You can change your choice at any time via "Cookie settings" in the footer.

## Who we share it with

We use these processors, under contracts that require them to protect your data:

- **HubSpot:** CRM, forms, meeting booking and (with consent) analytics
- **Cloudflare:** website hosting and security
- **WhatsApp (Meta):** if you choose to message us there

Some of our processors (HubSpot, Cloudflare and Meta) store or process data outside Indonesia, including in the United States. Before any transfer, we check that the receiving country offers personal data protection equal to or higher than UU PDP. Where it does not, we rely on binding data processing agreements with each processor that require adequate protection. Where neither applies, we ask for your consent before the transfer. These steps follow Article 56 of UU PDP and its implementing regulation, PP No. 33 Tahun 2026.

## How long we keep it

Enquiries that don’t become projects are deleted or anonymised after 24 months without contact. Analytics data is kept for up to 24 months. Server logs are kept only as long as our hosting provider needs them for security. Contracts, invoices and other client records are kept for 10 years, as Indonesian tax and company-document law requires. After that, we delete or anonymise them.
