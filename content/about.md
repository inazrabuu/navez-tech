## Why we exist

Growing companies in Indonesia usually buy technology in pieces: an agency for the website, a consultant for the ERP, a vendor for automation, and someone else for security. Each piece works on its own. Together, they rarely do, and the company pays for the gaps.

navez.tech is one team that covers all four. We design the website that brings in customers, set up the systems that run operations, build the AI agents that take on repetitive work, and secure all of it. Because we see the whole picture, the parts fit.

## How we work

- **Senior people on the work.** The people you meet on the first call are the people who build and run your project.
- **Phased and priced up front.** Every phase has a fixed scope and price in IDR, so you always know what you're buying.
- **You own everything.** Domains, accounts, code and data are set up in your company's name.
- **Security from the start.** Access control, backups and monitoring are part of every build, not an upsell.
